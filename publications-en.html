Publications and Articles

This page presents publications, articles, and documentary sources related to the professional work of Oren Shmuel as an Israeli folk dance choreographer and creator.

Rokdim Magazine

Hineh Shana Overet – Rokdim Magazine, Issue 61 (April 2003)

The dance "Hineh Shana Overet" was created by Oren Shmuel in the early 2000s and became one of the dances most closely associated with his work.

In April 2003, Rokdim Nirkoda Magazine published an article by Yaron Meishar, founder and editor of the magazine, documenting the background of the dance, its development, and Oren Shmuel's contribution to Israeli folk dance.

The article is preserved in the digital archive of the Association for Documentation and Preservation of Israeli Dance, which works to preserve and make accessible the history of Israeli folk dance for future generations.

Source: Yaron Meishar, "Hineh Shana Overet", Rokdim Nirkoda Magazine, Issue 61, April 2003.
The article is presented with the permission of author Yaron Meishar and courtesy of the digital archive of the Association for Documentation and Preservation of Israeli Dance.
