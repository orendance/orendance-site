Hine Shana Overet

The dance "Hine Shana Overet" was created by Oren Shmuel in the early 2000s and became one of the dances most closely associated with his choreographic work.

In April 2003, Rokdim Magazine published an article by Yaron Meishar, the magazine's founder and editor, documenting the background of the dance, its development, and Oren Shmuel's contribution to the field of Israeli folk dance.

The article is currently preserved in the digital archive of the Association for Documentation and Preservation of Israeli Dance, which works to preserve, document, and make accessible the history of Israeli folk dance for future generations.
